# Scrambled Eggs

## Ingredients

* eggs
* Salt
* Pepper
* 1 teaspoon of cooking oil

## Cooking Steps

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl.
2. Add salt and pepper, then whisk the eggs together.
3. Heat the cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.
4. Pour the eggs into the pan.
5. Stir the eggs gently until they are cooked.
6. Remove the scrambled eggs from the pan and serve.

**Cooking Tip:** Do not use high heat because it can make the eggs dry and overcooked.
